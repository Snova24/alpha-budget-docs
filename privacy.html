Alpha budget privacy policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026.

Alpha budget is a personal budget app. In version 1, your budget never leaves this device. Lines, balances, and notes are stored in local storage (or the app’s private files). We do not run ads, analytics, or crash beacons in this version.

We do not collect your name, email, bank credentials, or location. There is no account. JSON export is a file you choose to save.

Later upgrades (Ask Cal, Imagine themes, CSV import, linked banks, optional sign-in) will use a server and will update this page before they turn on. Those features are padlocked until then.

To wipe data: clear the app’s storage or uninstall. On the web, clear this site’s local storage.
